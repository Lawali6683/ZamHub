About the CEO

Malam Umar Maiwata is a Computer Scientist, Technology Entrepreneur, Crypto Strategist, and Web3 Solutions Specialist with a strong interest in digital innovation, blockchain technology, financial markets, and technology-driven entrepreneurship.

As the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of ZAMHUB NIG LTD, he leads the company's vision of building an integrated digital ecosystem that connects technology, education, entrepreneurship, and emerging digital opportunities.

His work focuses on creating practical opportunities for individuals and businesses through digital solutions, technology education, cryptocurrency trading education, decentralized finance (DeFi), blockchain innovation, and Web3 career development.

With a vision of making digital knowledge and opportunities more accessible, he is committed to helping young people develop relevant skills, understand emerging technologies, and participate more effectively in the global digital economy.

Technology & Digital Solutions

Through ZAMHUB, he promotes the use of technology to solve practical problems, improve business operations, and create new opportunities for individuals and organizations.

His vision encompasses digital services, technology-driven business solutions, and innovative platforms designed to meet the changing needs of the digital economy.

Cryptocurrency & Web3

As a Crypto Strategist and Web3 practitioner, he focuses on cryptocurrency market research, centralized and decentralized exchanges (CEX and DEX), on-chain analysis, trading strategies, blockchain education, and emerging Web3 opportunities.

His approach emphasizes research, risk management, discipline, continuous learning, and responsible participation in digital asset markets.

Digital Education & Empowerment

He believes that access to relevant digital education is essential for Africa's future.

Through digital learning initiatives and academy programmes, his vision is to support individuals in developing practical skills in cryptocurrency, blockchain technology, Web3, digital entrepreneurship, and other emerging technology fields.

His goal is to help learners move beyond theoretical knowledge and develop skills they can apply in real-world situations.

Entrepreneurship & Business Development

He is passionate about building sustainable businesses, developing innovative ideas, and creating platforms that connect people with knowledge, technology, and opportunities.

His leadership philosophy centres on innovation, strategic thinking, community empowerment, and long-term value creation.

Vision for ZAMHUB

To build ZAMHUB into a trusted African digital ecosystem where technology, education, entrepreneurship, blockchain innovation, and digital opportunities converge to empower individuals and businesses.

Leadership Philosophy

Empower people with knowledge, equip them with technology, and create opportunities that drive sustainable progress.
